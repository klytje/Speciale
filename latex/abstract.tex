\begin{center}
	\large
	\textbf{Abstract}
\end{center}
This thesis presents the first detailed Dalitz-plot analysis of the triple-alpha breakup of the \SI{17.76}{MeV} resonance in \ce{^12C}, and also somewhat shorter analyses of the nearby \SI{16.62}{MeV} and \SI{18.35}{MeV} resonances. The data was taken at the \SI{5}{MeV} accelerator at Aarhus University, which uses the \ce{^11B(p, \alpha)\alpha\alpha} reaction to populate the resonances. A sequential two-step model which accounts for interference between multiple partial waves is used to fit the Dalitz plots themselves with simulations. Furthermore, an angular correlation analysis is performed on only the \SI{17.76}{MeV} resonance. Both analyses agrees on the result: there is no trace of a \SI{17.76}{MeV} $0^+$ resonance on the $\alpha_1$ band. The analysis for the other two resonances are in agreement with previous studies.
